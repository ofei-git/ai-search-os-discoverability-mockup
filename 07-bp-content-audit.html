Lesson 2.5 Best Practice

Content audit for AI extraction

Written by Michael Ofei Reviewed by Kevin Indig Updated May 20, 2026 Read time ~4 minutes

The technical audit (the previous lesson) tells you whether AI systems can reach and read your site. The content audit tells you which of your pages are worth restructuring once they can. It's the content-side diagnostic — a triage that combines AI visibility scanning, top-performer analysis, competitor gap analysis, and extraction testing to produce a ranked list of the pages worth your editorial investment.

Why it works

Passage-level optimization, content formats, content refreshes — they're all real editorial work, and you can't do them everywhere at once. Without an audit, the content work becomes either paralyzing (you're staring at 1,200 pages) or arbitrary (you fix whatever's top of mind).

This lesson is the content-side diagnostic. The fixes it points to each have their own lesson — passage-level structure (Lesson 2.7), extractable formats (Lesson 2.11), freshness (Lesson 2.9). The audit's job is to rank your pages so you spend editorial effort where it moves citation share, not where it's easiest to start.

One technique does most of the work: extraction testing. Pulling the plain Markdown an AI agent would actually see from a page surfaces gaps that aren't visible in a normal browser1 — buried answers, content trapped in interactive components, key facts that never make it into the extracted text. Mike King's AI Search Manual lays out the broader generative-engine audit approach this builds on.2

How to apply

  • Scan your site for current AI visibility — which pages already earn citations, which don't.
  • Triage your top performers: which high-traffic, high-authority pages are weakest on extraction and structure?
  • Run a competitor gap analysis: where do competitors get cited and you don't?
  • Test extraction on priority pages — pull the Markdown an AI agent would actually see, and compare it to what a human sees.
  • Produce a ranked list. Start at the top, not everywhere.

Watch out for

  • The audit is diagnosis, not the fix. It tells you which pages to restructure; the how lives in the passage-level, formats, and freshness lessons.
  • Repeat it quarterly. Your content, your competitors, and the AI engines all move — a six-month-old audit is a stale map.
