How to act on Discoverability

Written by Michael Ofei Reviewed by Kevin Indig Updated May 20, 2026 Read time ~3 minutes

AI can only recommend what it can find. To turn that into a plan you can act on, we evaluated every tactic in this course against three criteria — chosen because they balance technical mechanism, independent evidence, and practical business value. Each was scored by the Semrush team and reviewed by Kevin Indig, our featured expert for Discoverability.

The tactics that cleared all three criteria became Best Practices — high-confidence moves you should commit to. The tactics that cleared two of three became Bets — promising work worth experimenting with, but with less certainty than a Best Practice. Build the high-confidence foundation first, then layer in the Bets where they fit your team's capacity and risk tolerance.

Our three criteria
1
LLM-grounded
It ties to how LLMs and AI retrieval actually work — we can explain the mechanism, not just guess.
2
Research-backed
Trusted research or industry data indicates it works — not just our own opinion.
3
Good for business
It's sound practice regardless of AI search — even if AI search disappeared tomorrow, you'd still do it.

Read the full methodology →

Best Practices in this course
  1. Technical SEO audit
    Audit the technical foundation AI Search inherits from traditional SEO.
  2. Content audit for AI extraction
    Identify which pages to prioritize before applying passage-level optimization.
  3. Make content extractable from HTML
    Ensure AI crawlers can see your content without executing JavaScript.
  4. Passage-level content optimization
    Structure content so chunks survive the RAG retrieval pipeline.
  5. Set a deliberate AI crawler policy
    Decide bot by bot whether each AI crawler should be allowed, blocked, or limited.
  6. Refresh high-authority content regularly
    Keep your most-cited pages current so AI systems retrieve fresh information.
  7. Conduct prompt research
    Identify the questions AI users ask in your category — then write to answer them.
Bets in this course
  1. AI-extractable content formats
    Build pages in formats AI surfaces structurally — comparison tables, FAQ structures, decision-tree content.
  2. Agent behavior / log analysis
    Mine server logs to understand how AI agents actually interact with your site.
