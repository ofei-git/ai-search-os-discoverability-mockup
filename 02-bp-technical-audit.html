Lesson 2.4 Best Practice

Technical SEO audit

Updated May 19, 2026 Read time ~4 minutes Layer Discoverability

The technical baseline AI Search inherits from SEO. Crawlability, indexability, server health, internal linking, Core Web Vitals — none of it is new, but it matters more now. If your foundation is broken, every AI-specific investment downstream has no surface to land on.

Why it works

Two angles. The mechanism: AI Search builds on the same crawl-and-index foundation as traditional search, so an unreachable or unindexable site stays invisible regardless of how AI-native your content strategy is. Google Search Central's guidance on AI features reinforces the point — the technical fundamentals that earn you traditional rankings are also what earns AI surfacing.

The leadership angle: this audit is also your single-page artifact for the budget conversation. It's how you walk into a senior meeting with a clear snapshot of where the foundation stands, what's blocking AI visibility today, and what investment would clear the runway for everything downstream. The audit isn't a backlog — it's a decision document.

This lesson is the diagnostic frame. Each AI-specific audit area below has a dedicated lesson later in the course — the audit identifies which to invest in first.

What you're auditing · traditional SEO
  • Crawlability (robots.txt, server response, blocked resources)
  • Indexability (sitemaps, canonicals, noindex)
  • Mobile rendering and responsive design
  • HTTPS, server uptime, response health
  • Internal linking from a discovery perspective
  • Core Web Vitals and page performance
What you're auditing · AI-specific
  • AI crawler policy, per bot → Lesson 2.8
  • Server-side rendering coverage on priority pages → Lesson 2.6
  • Passage-level extractability of key content → Lesson 2.7
  • AI bot access logs (which agents fetch what) → Lesson 2.12 (Bet)
  • Content freshness signals on cited pages → Lesson 2.9

How to apply

  • Run a full technical audit with Semrush Site Audit (or your equivalent). Don't rebuild the procedure from scratch — the tooling already does it better.
  • Layer the AI-specific checks on top: crawler policy (Lesson 2.8), SSR coverage (Lesson 2.6), passage extractability (Lesson 2.7), AI bot logs (Lesson 2.12 Bet).
  • Translate findings into a leadership artifact, not a backlog. Surface the 3-5 issues that matter most for senior conversation — leave the rest for the team's working tickets.
  • Treat it as a recurring posture. The foundation drifts.

Watch out for

  • Audit fatigue. If you produce a 200-row backlog nobody reads, the audit failed. Pick the 3-5 issues that move the needle for leadership and execution.
  • Diagnosis is different from strategy. The audit tells you where you stand; what you do about it is the strategy conversation that comes next.
